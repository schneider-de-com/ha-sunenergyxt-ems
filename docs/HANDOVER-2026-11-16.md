## 2. Release scope and explicit exclusions

- **v1.0.0 must deliver:** one reliable adaptive signed **whole-house net** grid controller with coordinated Deye M200/M80 / independently eligible BK215 stacks, individual SoC lower reserve, confirmed Deye limit writes, safe manual OFF/restart and failure recovery, user-selectable **no intentional export** versus **verified PV after full-charge export**, simple configuration and intelligible state. Correct abnormal power swings and avoid long-lived export-only lockout. Hardware truthfulness above low latency; no millisecond-speed inverter control required.
- **Not a 1.0.0 gate:** optional economic PV/demand *forecast optimization*. Preserve its decisions but defer activation and the [seven-day evaluation #92](https://github.com/schneider-de-com/ha-sunenergyxt-ems/issues/92) to a later 1.x release. Active dynamic-tariff dispatch ([#14](https://github.com/schneider-de-com/ha-sunenergyxt-ems/issues/14)) remains future-only. Do not claim b18 experimental planner is production-approved.
- **Do not add** individual-phase control/alarm requirements (approved Q15: net sum only); a software BK215 DC >1920 W shutdown latch (Q16 rejects it; 2077 W reading is not certified safe nameplate power); third-party writer attribution (Q17 rejects it); complex alert infrastructure (Q18 favors simple logged status); summer/winter mode switch (Q23 rejects it). Withdrawn Q24 duplicated already-decided Q2 — do **not** ask it again.
- Q10 manufacturer-based, PV-prioritized BMS calibration is agreed in principle, but interval X and actual BMS completion evidence **not confirmed**. Do not invent a timetable, force grid charge or claim it is included/validated merely to publish 1.0.

See the source Memory for all approved Q1–Q23 details; avoid another requirements questionnaire. Ask only about a genuinely unresolved release scope or physical hardware intervention.
